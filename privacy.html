Khadda

Privacy policy. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Khadda is a web page at this address for reporting potholes to the municipal authority responsible for the road. It is run by one person, not a company, and it has no server, no database and no accounts. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What Khadda collects, and where it goes

  • Your photo. It is resized on your phone and attached to the complaint email. It is not uploaded anywhere else.
  • Your location. The phone's GPS position, or the position stored in a photo you chose, is used to find the address and the responsible authority. The coordinates and a map link go in the email.
  • Your name and phone number. You type these once. They are saved in your browser's storage on your own phone and used only to sign the email. Officials usually need a name to act on a complaint. The phone number is optional.
  • Your sent reports. A short list of what you have sent, with the date, place and authority, is kept in your browser's storage on your phone so you can see it later. Nothing is sent to us.

Khadda has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Services the page talks to

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim receives your coordinates and returns an address. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Overpass API receives your coordinates and returns the names of nearby roads, so Khadda can tell if the road is a national or state highway.
  • Google provides the sign-in, the Gmail sending described below, and the fonts on the page.

How Khadda uses your Google account

When you tap send, Khadda asks Google for permission to send email on your behalf. This is the only Google permission it asks for, called gmail.send. With it, Khadda does exactly one thing: it sends the complaint you have just read and confirmed, from your own Gmail address, to the authority shown on the screen. A copy appears in your Gmail Sent folder, and any reply comes to your inbox.

Khadda cannot read your email, see your contacts, change your settings or delete anything. The permission token Google gives the page lives only in your browser's memory while the page is open, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Khadda's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Khadda's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who Google user data is shared with

Khadda does not share, transfer, sell or disclose Google user data to anyone. In plain terms:

  • The Gmail access token Google gives the page is used once, in your browser, to call the Gmail API. It is never sent to any other service, server or person, and it is never stored.
  • Your Google account details, such as your email address or name on the account, are not read by Khadda at all. The Gmail API fills in the From line itself.
  • The complaint email is written by you and sent by you. Its recipients are the ones shown on the screen before you confirm: the municipal authority, and where the page can identify them, the Chief Minister's Office and your MP and MLA. They receive the email, not any Google account data.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Overpass receive only coordinates, never anything from your Google account.
  • There are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services, so there is nobody else to share with.
  • Khadda holds no data on any server, so there is nothing to hand over in response to a legal request, a sale of the project, or anything else.

Khadda does not use Google user data to build or train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, and does not pass it to anyone who does.

How your data is protected

  • Encryption in transit. The page is served only over HTTPS, and every request it makes, to Google, Nominatim and Overpass, uses HTTPS.
  • No storage of Google data. The access token lives only in the page's memory while it is open. It is not written to browser storage, cookies, logs or any server. Closing the page discards it. Each send asks Google for a fresh, short-lived token that expires on its own within an hour.
  • Send-only access. Khadda asks for the gmail.send permission alone, so even the token it holds cannot read, search, change or delete your mail.
  • No server. There is no backend, database, account system or administrator with access to your information. Nothing is retained by us because nothing reaches us.
  • Your details stay on your phone. Your name, optional phone number and the list of reports you have sent are kept in your browser's storage on your own device, protected by your device's own lock and encryption, and removable at any time by clearing site data.
  • Minimal third-party code. The only outside scripts the page loads are Google Identity Services, for sign-in, and Google Fonts. No other code runs on the page.
  • Open source. The complete code is public at github.com/naman1911/khadda, so anyone can check that it does what this policy says.
  • Revocation. You can withdraw Khadda's access from your Google account at any time, and the page will simply ask again the next time you send.

Who receives your complaint

The email goes to the municipal corporation or council that Khadda matches to the address, using the public contact addresses listed in the project's authority list. You see the recipient, the subject and the full text before anything is sent, and you can change any of it. Once sent, the email is in the authority's hands and subject to their own rules.

Retention and deletion

Google user data is retained for no time at all: the access token is discarded when the send finishes or the page closes. Everything else Khadda keeps, your name, phone number and the list of reports, is in your browser's storage on your phone for as long as you choose. Clearing site data for this page in your browser settings removes all of it. There is nothing to delete on our side, because we hold nothing.

Children

Khadda is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions go to citizen@khadda.in.
